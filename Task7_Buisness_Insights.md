# Task 7 - Business Insights & Recommendations
## Business Insights

1. **Strong Overall Performance:** The dashboard shows approximately 9.75M in total sales and 5M units sold.

2. **Monthly Trend:** Sales generally increased from June to November 2011, with November showing the highest performance. December is lower because the dataset contains only partial December data.

3. **Top Market:** The United Kingdom is the strongest market and contributes the highest sales compared with other countries.

4. **Popular Products:** Products such as DOTCOM POSTAGE, REGENCY CAKES, WHITE HANGING HEART T-LIGHT HOLDER and JUMBO BAG RED RETROSPOT are among the top-selling products.

5. **Price and Quantity Relationship:** The correlation between Quantity and UnitPrice is -0.003788, showing almost no linear relationship between price and quantity sold.

## Business Recommendations
1. **Improve Inventory Planning:** Maintain sufficient stock of top-selling products, especially before high-demand periods such as November, to avoid stockouts.

2. **Expand Lower-Performing Markets:** Continue focusing on the UK while using targeted promotions and improved delivery offers to increase sales in other countries.

## Conclusion
The analysis identifies important sales trends, popular products and key markets. These insights can support better inventory planning, marketing and business growth.
